Module 6

Educator Support

Inclusive classroom strategies

Design learning activities that allow multiple ways to participate, respond, and demonstrate understanding.

Differentiated learning

Adjust content, process, and output to match different learner needs.

Clear instructions

Use short, direct instructions and model examples before independent work.

Break tasks into steps

Support learners by dividing a task into manageable chunks and checking each part.

Extra time where appropriate

Provide reasonable adjustments that reduce stress without lowering expectations.

Alternative ways of demonstrating learning

Allow oral responses, diagrams, projects, or visual explanations where helpful.

Reading and writing support

Offer structured text, guided reading, vocabulary support, and visual organizers.

Positive classroom communication

Use encouraging language that focuses on effort, progress, and strengths.

Parent-teacher collaboration

Share observations and build a shared understanding of the learner’s needs and support plan.
