Learning Areas

Reading

Reading may involve difficulty with decoding words, recognizing patterns, or understanding meaning.

  • Possible difficulties: slow reading, misreading words, poor sentence understanding.
  • Helpful strategies: guided reading, chunking words, repeated practice with simple texts.
  • When to seek guidance: persistent difficulty across reading tasks.

Writing

Writing may require planning, organizing, and expressing ideas clearly on paper.

  • Possible difficulties: messy ideas, missing structure, slow writing speed.
  • Helpful strategies: graphic organizers, sentence starters, chunking ideas.
  • When to seek guidance: writing remains difficult despite support.

Spelling

Spelling may involve remembering letter patterns, sounds, and word forms.

  • Possible difficulties: missing letters, confusion with similar words.
  • Helpful strategies: sound-based practice, word families, writing in context.
  • When to seek guidance: spelling errors are frequent and interfere with learning.

Comprehension

Comprehension may involve processing spoken or written information and understanding meaning.

  • Possible difficulties: misunderstanding tasks, forgetting instructions, weak recall.
  • Helpful strategies: teach key words, ask oral questions, encourage summaries.
  • When to seek guidance: understanding remains weak despite repeated explanations.

Mathematics

Mathematics may involve number sense, memory of facts, sequence, and problem-solving.

  • Possible difficulties: confusion with number symbols, weak calculation fluency.
  • Helpful strategies: use manipulatives, visual aids, step-by-step methods.
  • When to seek guidance: difficulties remain persistent across time and tasks.

Important reminder

These areas are examples of possible learning challenges. Difficulties in these areas do not automatically mean a learner has a learning disability. They may also reflect temporary issues, language differences, teaching mismatch, or environmental stress.

A learner should not be labeled based only on these difficulties. If concerns persist, consult a qualified professional.
