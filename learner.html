Module 4

Learner Support

Study Planning

Break the week into manageable tasks and review goals regularly.

Chunking Tasks

Divide large tasks into smaller steps to reduce overwhelm.

Reading Strategies

Use highlighting, previewing headings, and repeated reading when needed.

Writing Strategies

Plan with bullet points, sentence frames, and checking for clarity.

Spelling Practice

Practice word families, write in context, and build confidence gradually.

Mathematics Strategies

Use visual examples, formulas, and step-by-step problem solving.

Memory Techniques

Use mnemonics, revision cards, and repeated recall practice.

Time Management

Create routines for homework, revision, and rest.

Exam Preparation

Practice with sample questions, review key concepts, and take short breaks.

Taking Breaks

Short breaks help focus and reduce fatigue during difficult tasks.

Ask for Help

It is okay to ask a teacher or caregiver for support and clarification.
