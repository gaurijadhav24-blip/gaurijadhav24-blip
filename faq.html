Frequently Asked Questions

What is a learning disability?

A learning disability is a difference in how a person processes and learns information. It may affect reading, writing, spelling, comprehension, or mathematics. Learning disabilities do not reflect intelligence.

Does a learning disability mean low intelligence?

No. Many people with learning disabilities are bright and capable. They may simply learn in different ways or need support in specific areas while being strong in others.

What are the common learning areas affected?

Common areas include reading, writing, spelling, comprehension, and mathematics. However, individuals may experience difficulty in one area while being strong in another.

Who can assess learning difficulties?

Qualified professionals such as educational psychologists, special educators, clinical psychologists, and speech-language pathologists can assess learning difficulties.

Can this website diagnose a learning disability?

No. This portal is educational and informational only. It does not diagnose learning disabilities or replace professional assessment.

How can parents support a child with learning difficulties?

Parents can support by creating a positive learning environment, using clear communication, breaking tasks into smaller steps, and working collaboratively with teachers. Avoid negative labels and focus on strengths.

How can teachers support learners with difficulties?

Teachers can use inclusive classroom strategies, differentiated learning, clear instructions, extra time where appropriate, and alternative ways of demonstrating learning.

When should professional help be considered?

When difficulties persist over time and across settings despite classroom and home support, it is reasonable to seek professional guidance.

Is this a substitute for professional assessment?

No. This platform provides awareness and information. For formal assessment and diagnosis, you must consult a qualified professional.

How can I provide feedback?

You can share feedback using the Feedback form. We value your suggestions for improving the platform.
