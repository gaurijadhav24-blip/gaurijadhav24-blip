What this process means

When difficulties are consistent over time and affect learning, it is reasonable to speak with a teacher, parent, or another trusted adult. The goal is to better understand the learner’s needs and gather information about support.

Professional assessment can help identify strengths, challenges, and suitable strategies. It can also guide classroom adaptations and educational planning.

Important

The website does not perform diagnosis or replace professional assessment.

Key points to remember

  • Persistent difficulty across tasks and time may need attention.
  • Support should be individualized and strengths-based.
  • Assessment is a tool to guide support, not a label.
  • Parents, educators, and professionals should work together.
